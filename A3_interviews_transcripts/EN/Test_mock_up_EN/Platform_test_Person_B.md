Or, if it's urgent like here, I dig, and if I don't find it, I ask Person A. Well, here, I suppose it's the panel on the right that's supposed to help me.

"Copilot, account pre-read. It proposes; you check and you send. It never sends anything to the merchant and never moves money." OK, so it's me who sends, OK. "No open flag on this account". Then, "proposed transaction", "confident match". Hmm. Confident, does that mean it's sure? Sure how much? The amount, 1,200, and she says about 1,200. The date, 3 November, within last week. The payer, Dupont, Dupont Rénovation. And it's the only payment from this payer over 30 days. OK. Well, there, that reassures me a bit, I see why.

But me, I still want to check. Because that's what I was taught: you always check. So, where do I open to go check? I don't see... There's "confirm this transaction" and "not this one". Well, maybe if I click on "not this one", I get access. I'll try. ... Ah. "No transaction selected. The ticket works as it does today: search the merchant's ledger." And there, there's "open the merchant's ledger". Oh no, but that's not what I wanted. Me, I didn't want to say it wasn't that one, I just wanted to check. Well, there's "back to the proposal", I go back. Phew. There, it's back, it's still "confident match". That, I think that, without meaning to, you can say no when you just wanted to look. It's a shame, if I want to check I still have to go open the tools in the end. Well, at least I already have all the info, so that already saves me time, that.

Well, I'm going to look at the "2 other candidates ruled out". There's Dupuis et Fils, 1,150, not the same name. And TX-88021, 1,200 euros, 20 October, Dupont Rénovation too. **Interviewer**

Thanks. The test is over. What made you hesitate?

**Person B**

The "not this one", when I just wanted to check. I was scared I'd broken something. There should be a "check" button next to it, that doesn't say no. And the "confident", I didn't really know how far I could trust it. Then, with the "why this one", it's OK.

**Interviewer**

The last time you had a ticket like this one, how did it go?

**Person B**

Last week, I had one, a gentleman who said he hadn't received a payment, without a reference either. I searched for a while, I couldn't find the amount. Actually, it was also a grouped transfer, it was Person A who told me. It took me, I don't know, forty minutes, with the time to ask her. Here, I took five minutes, and even then, because I clicked in the wrong place.

**Interviewer**

What did you expect to see instead?

**Person B**

A button to open the transaction directly to check it, without saying no. And maybe a percentage, or a level, next to "confident". And then... me, what I'm wondering is when it doesn't find it. Here, she put Dupont. But often, it's "my payment from last week" and nothing else. I'd really like to see what it does in that case. If it tells me what question to ask the customer, that would already help me, because sometimes, I don't even know what to ask him, or I don't understand what the customer is telling me.

**Interviewer**

Is there anything else you want to add?

**Person B**

No... Just that I would have really liked to have this when I started. My first ticket, an hour. With this, I think I would have gone faster, and I would have bothered Person A less. It's really great, your tool, it's going to help quite a lot of people, that, if it's reliable enough.

OK, thanks a lot. We'll stop there for Person B's test.
